# AdVantage Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to AdVantage!

This quick start guide will help you get up and running with AdVantage, your AI-powered social commerce platform, in just a few minutes.

## 1. Installation

### iOS
1. Open the App Store on your iOS device
2. Search for "AdVantage"
3. Tap "Get" or the download icon
4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
5. Wait for the app to download and install

### Android
1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device
2. Search for "AdVantage"
3. Tap "Install"
4. Wait for the app to download and install

## 2. Account Creation

1. Open the AdVantage app
2. Tap "Register" on the welcome screen
3. Enter your:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (8+ characters with letters, numbers, and symbols)
4. Tap "Register"
5. Check your email for a verification link
6. Tap the verification link to activate your account

## 3. First Login

1. Open the AdVantage app
2. Enter your email and password
3. Tap "Login"
4. (Optional) Enable biometric login when prompted for faster future access

## 4. Complete Your Profile

1. Tap "Complete Profile" on the welcome dashboard
2. Add your business information:
   - Business name
   - Industry (select from options)
   - Website (optional)
   - Business description
3. Upload your business logo or profile picture
4. Tap "Save" to continue

## 5. Connect Social Media Accounts

1. Navigate to the Profile tab
2. Tap "Connected Accounts"
3. Select the platforms you want to connect:
   - Facebook
   - Instagram
   - Twitter
   - LinkedIn
   - TikTok
   - YouTube
4. Follow the authentication steps for each platform
5. Return to the AdVantage app when complete

## 6. Create Your First Campaign

1. Tap the "+" button on the Dashboard or Campaigns tab
2. Select "Quick Campaign" for a guided setup
3. Choose a campaign objective:
   - Awareness
   - Engagement
   - Conversions
4. Select target platforms
5. Let the AI suggest content or upload your own
6. Review campaign settings
7. Tap "Launch Campaign"

## 7. Use the AI Agent

1. Navigate to the AI Agent tab
2. Type a question or request like:
   - "Create a post about our new product"
   - "Suggest hashtags for a fashion post"
   - "Analyze my recent campaign performance"
3. Review the AI's response
4. Tap "Use This" to apply suggestions directly to campaigns or save to your content library

## 8. Check Your Analytics

1. Navigate to the Analytics tab
2. View your performance overview
3. Tap on specific metrics to see detailed breakdowns
4. Use the date selector to change the time period
5. Tap "Export" to share reports

## 9. Set Up Notifications

1. Navigate to the Profile tab
2. Tap "Settings"
3. Tap "Notifications"
4. Choose which notifications you want to receive:
   - Campaign performance alerts
   - AI insights
   - Content recommendations
   - Platform updates
5. Tap "Save" to apply your preferences

## 10. Explore Advanced Features

Once you're comfortable with the basics, explore these advanced features:

- **Multi-Platform Campaigns**: Create coordinated campaigns across multiple platforms
- **Content Library**: Save and organize your content for reuse
- **Team Collaboration**: Invite team members to collaborate on campaigns
- **Custom Reports**: Create tailored analytics reports
- **Automation Rules**: Set up triggers for automatic campaign adjustments

## Need Help?

- Tap the "?" icon in the top corner of any screen for contextual help
- Visit our knowledge base at help.advantage-app.com
- Contact support at support@advantage-app.com
- Chat with us in-app via Profile > Help > Chat with Support

## Quick Tips

- 💡 Use the AI Agent for content ideas when you're stuck
- 📊 Check your analytics weekly to optimize performance
- 🔄 Connect all your social accounts for best results
- 📱 Enable notifications for important alerts
- 🎯 Start with smaller campaigns to learn the platform
- 📅 Use the content calendar to plan ahead
- 🔍 Explore the dashboard widgets for quick insights

Congratulations! You're now ready to start using AdVantage to supercharge your social media marketing. Happy campaigning!
