## Getting Started

### Installation

1. **iOS**: Download AdVantage from the App Store.
2. **Android**: Download AdVantage from the Google Play Store.

### Registration

1. Open the AdVantage app.
2. Tap "Register" on the welcome screen.
3. Fill in your details:
   - Name
   - Email address
   - Password (must be at least 8 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols)
4. Tap "Register" to create your account.
5. Check your email for a verification link and follow the instructions.

### Login

1. Open the AdVantage app.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Tap "Login" to access your account.
4. (Optional) Enable biometric authentication (Face ID or Fingerprint) for faster login in the future.

### Onboarding

After your first login, you'll be guided through an onboarding process:

1. **Business Profile**: Set up your business profile with industry, name, and description.
2. **Platform Connections**: Connect your social media accounts.
3. **Goals**: Define your marketing goals.
4. **Preferences**: Set your preferences for AI assistance and notifications.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is your central hub for monitoring your marketing performance and accessing key features.

### Key Components

- **Metrics Cards**: View key performance metrics like impressions, engagement, and conversions.
- **Campaign Cards**: See your active campaigns and their performance.
- **AI Insights**: Get AI-powered recommendations for improving your marketing.
- **Quick Actions**: Access common actions like creating a new campaign or analyzing performance.

### Navigation

- Use the bottom tab bar to navigate between main sections:
  - Dashboard
  - Campaigns
  - AI Agent
  - Analytics
  - Profile

## Campaign Management

Create and manage your marketing campaigns across multiple platforms.

### Creating a Campaign

1. Tap the "+" button on the Campaigns tab or use the "Create Campaign" quick action on the Dashboard.
2. Select a campaign type:
   - Social Media Campaign
   - Multi-Platform Campaign
   - Email Campaign
   - Event Campaign
3. Fill in the campaign details:
   - Name
   - Description
   - Start and end dates
   - Budget
   - Objectives
4. Select target platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.).
5. Create or select content for your campaign.
6. Review and launch your campaign.

### Managing Campaigns

- **View Campaigns**: See all your campaigns on the Campaigns tab.
- **Filter Campaigns**: Filter by status, platform, or date range.
- **Edit Campaign**: Tap on a campaign and select "Edit" to modify details.
- **Pause/Resume Campaign**: Toggle the status switch on a campaign card.
- **Delete Campaign**: Tap on a campaign, select "More Options", and choose "Delete".

### Campaign Analytics

- Tap on a campaign to view detailed analytics:
  - Impressions
  - Engagement
  - Clicks
  - Conversions
  - ROI
  - Audience demographics

## AI Agent

The AI Agent is your personal marketing assistant that helps you create and optimize campaigns.

### Starting a Conversation

1. Navigate to the AI Agent tab.
2. Type your question or request in the chat input.
3. Tap "Send" to get a response from the AI Agent.

### Example Requests

- "Create a social media campaign for my new product launch"
- "Analyze my recent campaign performance"
- "Suggest content ideas for my Instagram"
- "Optimize my Facebook ad targeting"
- "Write a caption for this product image"

### Using AI-Generated Content

1. When the AI suggests content, you'll see a "Use This" button.
2. Tap "Use This" to apply the content to your campaign or save it to your content library.
3. You can edit the content before using it by tapping "Edit".

## Analytics

The Analytics section provides detailed insights into your marketing performance.

### Overview

- **Performance Summary**: See your overall marketing performance.
- **Platform Breakdown**: Compare performance across different platforms.
- **Audience Insights**: Understand your audience demographics and behavior.
- **Content Performance**: See which content types perform best.

### Custom Reports

1. Tap "Create Report" in the Analytics tab.
2. Select metrics to include.
3. Choose a date range.
4. Select platforms to include.
5. Tap "Generate Report" to create your custom report.
6. Use the "Export" button to share or save your report.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Issues

- **Forgot Password**: Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen and follow the instructions.
- **Account Locked**: If your account is locked, contact support at support@advantage-app.com.

#### App Performance

- **Slow Performance**: Clear the app cache in Settings > Storage > Clear Cache.
- **Crashes**: Update to the latest version of the app.

#### Connectivity Issues

- **Offline Mode**: The app will work in offline mode and sync when you're back online.
- **Sync Issues**: If data isn't syncing, go to Settings > Advanced > Sync Data.

### Getting Help

- **In-App Help**: Tap the "?" icon in the top right corner of any screen.
- **Support Chat**: Use the chat feature in the Profile > Help section.
- **Email Support**: Contact support@advantage-app.com.
- **Knowledge Base**: Visit help.advantage-app.com for articles and tutorials.
