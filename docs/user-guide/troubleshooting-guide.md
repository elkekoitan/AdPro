## Installation Issues

### App Won't Download

**Issue**: Unable to download the app from the App Store or Google Play Store.

**Solutions**:
- Check your internet connection.
- Ensure you have enough storage space on your device.
- Verify that your device meets the minimum requirements:
  - iOS 13 or later
  - Android 9 or later
- Try restarting your device and attempting the download again.

### App Won't Install

**Issue**: The app downloads but fails to install.

**Solutions**:
- Restart your device and try again.
- Check for any pending OS updates.
- Clear the app store cache:
  - **iOS**: Tap the App Store icon 10 times quickly.
  - **Android**: Go to Settings > Apps > Google Play Store > Storage > Clear Cache.
- Uninstall and reinstall the app.

### App Won't Update

**Issue**: Unable to update to the latest version.

**Solutions**:
- Check your internet connection.
- Ensure you have enough storage space.
- Close the app store and reopen it.
- Restart your device and try again.

## Login and Authentication Issues

### Forgot Password

**Issue**: Unable to log in due to forgotten password.

**Solutions**:
1. Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Check your email for password reset instructions.
4. Follow the link to reset your password.
5. Create a new password and log in.

### Account Locked

**Issue**: Account is locked after multiple failed login attempts.

**Solutions**:
1. Wait 30 minutes before trying again.
2. Use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password.
3. Contact support if the issue persists.

### Email Verification

**Issue**: Not receiving email verification link.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your spam or junk folder.
2. Verify that you entered the correct email address.
3. Tap "Resend Verification Email" on the verification screen.
4. Add no-reply@advantage-app.com to your contacts.
5. Contact support if the issue persists.

### Biometric Authentication Not Working

**Issue**: Face ID or Fingerprint authentication not working.

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure biometric authentication is enabled in your device settings.
2. Go to AdVantage Settings > Security and toggle biometric authentication off and on.
3. Re-register your biometric data in your device settings.
4. Use your password to log in and set up biometric authentication again.

## Performance Issues

### App Running Slowly

**Issue**: The app is sluggish or unresponsive.

**Solutions**:
1. Close other apps running in the background.
2. Restart the app.
3. Clear the app cache:
   - **iOS**: Settings > General > iPhone Storage > AdVantage > Offload App
   - **Android**: Settings > Apps > AdVantage > Storage > Clear Cache
4. Ensure your device has sufficient free storage.
5. Update to the latest version of the app.
6. Restart your device.

### High Battery Usage

**Issue**: The app is consuming excessive battery power.

**Solutions**:
1. Check if the app is running in the background when not in use.
2. Go to Settings > Notifications and adjust notification frequency.
3. Disable background refresh for the app in device settings.
4. Update to the latest version of the app.
5. Contact support if the issue persists.

### High Data Usage

**Issue**: The app is using too much mobile data.

**Solutions**:
1. Go to Settings > Data Usage and enable "Data Saver" mode.
2. Disable automatic media downloads over cellular data.
3. Reduce analytics data collection in Settings > Privacy.
4. Use the app primarily on Wi-Fi.

## Connectivity Issues

### Unable to Connect to Server

**Issue**: App displays "Unable to connect to server" error.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Try switching between Wi-Fi and cellular data.
3. Restart the app.
4. Check if the server is down by visiting status.advantage-app.com.
5. Try again later if it's a server-side issue.

### Offline Mode Not Working

**Issue**: App doesn't work properly in offline mode.

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure you've previously logged in while online.
2. Check if offline mode is enabled in Settings > Advanced > Offline Mode.
3. Some features require an internet connection and won't work offline.
4. Sync your data when you're back online by pulling down to refresh.

### Sync Issues

**Issue**: Changes made offline aren't syncing when back online.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Pull down to refresh on the main screens.
3. Go to Settings > Advanced > Sync Data and tap "Force Sync".
4. Check for sync conflicts in Settings > Advanced > Sync Conflicts.
5. Restart the app and try again.

## Campaign Management Issues

### Unable to Create Campaign

**Issue**: Error when trying to create a new campaign.

**Solutions**:
1. Check all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions for your account type.
3. Verify that you're connected to the internet.
4. Check if you've reached your campaign limit (based on your subscription).
5. Try creating a simpler campaign first.

### Campaign Not Publishing

**Issue**: Campaign created but not publishing to social platforms.

**Solutions**:
1. Check the campaign status in the Campaigns tab.
2. Verify that your social media accounts are properly connected.
3. Check for any authorization issues with your social media accounts.
4. Ensure the campaign is scheduled for the current or future date.
5. Check for any content policy violations in your campaign.

### Unable to Edit Campaign

**Issue**: Cannot edit an existing campaign.

**Solutions**:
1. Check if the campaign is currently active or scheduled.
2. Some campaign properties cannot be edited after publishing.
3. Try pausing the campaign before editing.
4. Check your permissions if you're part of a team.
5. Create a duplicate campaign with your desired changes.

## AI Agent Issues

### AI Not Responding

**Issue**: AI Agent doesn't respond to queries.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Try restarting the conversation.
3. Keep your queries clear and specific.
4. Check if you've reached your AI usage limit.
5. Update to the latest version of the app.

### Poor AI Recommendations

**Issue**: AI recommendations are not relevant or helpful.

**Solutions**:
1. Provide more context in your queries.
2. Complete your business profile with detailed information.
3. Use the feedback buttons to improve AI responses.
4. Try rephrasing your question.
5. Train the AI by using it more frequently.

### AI Content Generation Issues

**Issue**: AI-generated content is not appropriate or relevant.

**Solutions**:
1. Provide more specific instructions.
2. Check your content guidelines in Settings > AI Preferences.
3. Use the "Regenerate" button to get alternative content.
4. Edit the generated content manually.
5. Provide feedback to improve future generations.

## Analytics Issues

### Analytics Not Loading

**Issue**: Analytics data is not loading or displaying.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Verify that you have campaigns with data to analyze.
3. Try selecting a different date range.
4. Clear the app cache and try again.
5. Update to the latest version of the app.

### Incorrect Analytics Data

**Issue**: Analytics data appears incorrect or inconsistent.

**Solutions**:
1. Check the date range selected.
2. Allow 24-48 hours for data to fully process.
3. Compare with data from the native platform analytics.
4. Check for any filters that might be affecting the data.
5. Contact support if the discrepancy is significant.

### Unable to Export Reports

**Issue**: Cannot export analytics reports.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Verify that you have the necessary permissions.
3. Try exporting a smaller date range.
4. Check if you've reached your export limit.
5. Try a different export format.

## App Crashes

### App Crashes on Launch

**Issue**: App closes immediately after opening.

**Solutions**:
1. Restart your device.
2. Update to the latest version of the app.
3. Reinstall the app.
4. Check for OS updates.
5. Clear app data (note: this will log you out):
   - **iOS**: Settings > General > iPhone Storage > AdVantage > Delete App
   - **Android**: Settings > Apps > AdVantage > Storage > Clear Data

### App Crashes During Use

**Issue**: App closes unexpectedly during use.

**Solutions**:
1. Note what you were doing when the crash occurred.
2. Update to the latest version of the app.
3. Close other apps running in the background.
4. Clear the app cache.
5. Report the crash through Settings > Help > Report a Problem.

### App Freezes

**Issue**: App becomes unresponsive but doesn't crash.

**Solutions**:
1. Force close the app and reopen it.
2. Restart your device.
3. Check for app updates.
4. Clear the app cache.
5. Reinstall the app if the issue persists.

## Data Synchronization Issues

### Changes Not Syncing

**Issue**: Changes made on one device not appearing on another.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection on both devices.
2. Pull down to refresh on both devices.
3. Ensure you're logged into the same account on both devices.
4. Go to Settings > Advanced > Sync Data and tap "Force Sync".
5. Allow some time for synchronization to complete.

### Duplicate Content

**Issue**: Content appears duplicated after syncing.

**Solutions**:
1. Check for and resolve sync conflicts in Settings > Advanced > Sync Conflicts.
2. Avoid making changes to the same item on multiple devices simultaneously.
3. Delete duplicate items manually.
4. Contact support if the issue persists.

### Lost Data

**Issue**: Data appears to be missing after syncing.

**Solutions**:
1. Check different views and filters to ensure the data isn't just filtered out.
2. Check the archive in case the data was archived.
3. Go to Settings > Advanced > Sync Data > Sync History to check for recent sync activities.
4. Contact support for data recovery assistance.
