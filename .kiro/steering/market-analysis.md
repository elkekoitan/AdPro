## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Market Risks
1. **Big Tech Competition:** Google, Meta entering space
   - **Mitigation:** Focus on SMB niche, faster innovation
2. **Economic Downturn:** Reduced marketing budgets
   - **Mitigation:** Emphasize ROI, cost savings
3. **AI Regulation:** Potential AI usage restrictions
   - **Mitigation:** Transparent AI, user control options

### Technical Risks
1. **AI Model Costs:** High OpenAI/Claude costs
   - **Mitigation:** Optimize usage, consider open-source models
2. **Platform API Changes:** Social media API restrictions
   - **Mitigation:** Diversified platform strategy, direct integrations
3. **Scalability:** High user growth challenges
   - **Mitigation:** Cloud-native architecture, auto-scaling
