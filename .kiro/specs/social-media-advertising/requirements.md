# Social Media Advertising - Requirements Document

## 🎯 **INTRODUCTION**

The Social Media Advertising module enables comprehensive paid advertising campaign management across all major social media platforms with AI-powered optimization, automated bid management, and unified campaign analytics.

**Strategic Importance:** Paid advertising represents 60% of social media marketing spend and higher-value customer segment with 2x ARPU potential.

---

## 📋 **REQUIREMENTS**

### **Requirement 1: Multi-Platform Ad Campaign Management**

**User Story:** As a paid media manager, I want unified campaign management across Facebook, Instagram, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Twitter, so I can efficiently manage all my paid advertising from one platform.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**

1. **WHEN** campaigns are created **THEN** system SHALL support Facebook Ads Manager integration with complete campaign, ad set, and ad management
2. **WHEN** Google Ads integration is used **THEN** system SHALL manage search, display, video, and shopping campaigns with keyword management
3. **WHEN** TikTok advertising is configured **THEN** system SHALL create video ads with trending sound integration and algorithm optimization
4. **WHEN** LinkedIn campaigns are managed **THEN** system SHALL support professional targeting and B2B campaign optimization
5. **WHEN** cross-platform campaigns are launched **THEN** system SHALL coordinate timing, budgets, and messaging across all platforms
6. **WHEN** campaign performance is tracked **THEN** system SHALL provide unified reporting with platform-specific metrics and insights

### **Requirement 2: AI-Powered Campaign Optimization**

**User Story:** As an advertiser seeking maximum ROI, I want AI-powered optimization that automatically adjusts bids, budgets, audiences, and creative elements to improve campaign performance continuously.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**

1. **WHEN** automated bidding is enabled **THEN** system SHALL optimize bids in real-time based on performance goals and budget constraints
2. **WHEN** audience optimization is active **THEN** system SHALL automatically expand or narrow audiences based on conversion data and lookalike performance
3. **WHEN** creative optimization is configured **THEN** system SHALL test ad variations and automatically promote best-performing creative elements
4. **WHEN** budget optimization is enabled **THEN** system SHALL reallocate budgets across campaigns and platforms based on performance efficiency
5. **WHEN** dayparting optimization is active **THEN** system SHALL adjust ad scheduling based on conversion patterns and audience activity
6. **WHEN** performance thresholds are set **THEN** system SHALL automatically pause underperforming ads and scale winning campaigns

### **Requirement 3: Advanced Audience Targeting and Lookalikes**

**User Story:** As a targeting specialist, I want sophisticated audience creation and management capabilities that leverage first-party data and AI to reach the most valuable prospects.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**

1. **WHEN** custom audiences are created **THEN** system SHALL build audiences from CRM data, website visitors, app users, and engagement data
2. **WHEN** lookalike audiences are generated **THEN** system SHALL create high-quality lookalikes with performance tracking and optimization
3. **WHEN** behavioral targeting is configured **THEN** system SHALL target based on purchase intent, content engagement, and interaction patterns
4. **WHEN** demographic targeting is applied **THEN** system SHALL provide detailed demographic and psychographic targeting options
5. **WHEN** interest targeting is used **THEN** system SHALL leverage platform interest data and third-party data sources
6. **WHEN** retargeting campaigns are launched **THEN** system SHALL create dynamic retargeting with product catalogs and personalized messaging

### **Requirement 4: Creative Asset Management and Testing**

**User Story:** As a creative manager, I want comprehensive creative asset management with automated A/B testing and performance optimization, so I can efficiently manage and optimize ad creative at scale.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**

1. **WHEN** creative assets are uploaded **THEN** system SHALL organize images, videos, copy variants, and templates with tagging and search capabilities
2. **WHEN** dynamic creative is configured **THEN** system SHALL automatically combine creative elements and test performance variations
3. **WHEN** A/B testing is enabled **THEN** system SHALL test creative variations with statistical significance and winner selection
4. **WHEN** creative performance is analyzed **THEN** system SHALL identify top-performing creative elements and suggest optimization
5. **WHEN** brand compliance is required **THEN** system SHALL check creative assets against brand guidelines and platform policies
6. **WHEN** creative scaling is needed **THEN** system SHALL generate variations of winning creative with AI-powered adaptation

### **Requirement 5: Comprehensive Attribution and ROI Analysis**

**User Story:** As a marketing director, I want accurate attribution and ROI analysis that shows the true impact of paid advertising on revenue and business outcomes.

#### **Acceptance Criteria**

1. **WHEN** conversion tracking is configured **THEN** system SHALL track conversions across platforms with accurate attribution and deduplication
2. **WHEN** ROI analysis is performed **THEN** system SHALL calculate ROAS, CPA, LTV, and incremental revenue impact
3. **WHEN** attribution modeling is applied **THEN** system SHALL support multiple attribution models with custom attribution windows
4. **WHEN** cross-device tracking is enabled **THEN** system SHALL track user journeys across devices and touchpoints
5. **WHEN** view-through attribution is measured **THEN** system SHALL track view-through conversions and assisted conversions
6. **WHEN** incrementality testing is conducted **THEN** system SHALL measure true incremental impact using control groups and statistical testing
