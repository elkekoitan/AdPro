# Advanced Analytics & Reporting - Requirements Document

## 🎯 **INTRODUCTION**

The Advanced Analytics & Reporting module transforms AdVantage from a basic social media management tool into a comprehensive marketing intelligence platform. This enterprise-grade analytics system provides AI-powered insights, predictive analytics, custom reporting, and advanced data visualization capabilities that enable data-driven marketing decisions.

**Strategic Importance:** This module is essential for enterprise sales and premium tier justification, providing the analytical depth that distinguishes AdVantage from basic social media tools. It enables sophisticated marketing measurement and ROI optimization that enterprises demand.

**Market Positioning:** Positions AdVantage as a complete marketing intelligence platform rather than just a social media tool, competing with enterprise analytics solutions while maintaining user-friendly design.

2. **WHEN** trend patterns are detected **THEN** the system SHALL predict future performance changes with early warning alerts sent 24-48 hours before significant impacts
3. **WHEN** seasonal patterns are identified **THEN** the system SHALL forecast seasonal performance variations and suggest preparation strategies with specific action recommendations
4. **WHEN** market conditions change **THEN** the system SHALL adjust predictions based on external factors including competitive landscape, economic indicators, and platform algorithm changes
5. **WHEN** prediction accuracy is measured **THEN** the system SHALL continuously improve forecasting models based on actual outcomes with weekly model retraining
6. **WHEN** multiple scenarios are evaluated **THEN** the system SHALL provide what-if analysis with budget allocation recommendations and ROI projections for different strategy options
7. **WHEN** anomaly detection is triggered **THEN** the system SHALL identify performance anomalies within 15 minutes and provide root cause analysis with correction recommendations
8. **WHEN** predictive insights are generated **THEN** the system SHALL provide confidence scores and explain reasoning behind predictions using transparent AI methodology
